**Effective Date:** January 01, 2027

This Privacy Policy explains how Shinkly Private Limited and its subsidiaries ("LogChimp," "we", "us" and "our") collect, use and share information, including information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person ("personal data" or "personal information") from our customers or users ("you" and "your") when you use, or demo, LogChimp's products such as LogChimp Self-Managed Software, or any other services maintained by LogChimp for use by our customers or users, such as Support Services (together the "Products").

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

**Account Information**

- Name
- Email address
- Company name (optional)

**Billing Information**

Billing details necessary to process payments
*(Note: Payment details are handled by third-party payment processors; LogChimp does not store full payment card data.)*

**Technical Information**
- IP address
- Browser and device information
- Usage logs related to the dashboard

**Product Telemetry (Self-Managed)**

When using LogChimp Self-Managed, we automatically collect minimal, privacy-conscious product telemetry to understand software adoption, improve the product, and maintain license integrity. This may include:

- Unique installation identifiers
- License-related identifiers and validation information
- Software versions
- Deployment environments
- Aggregated workspace and user counts
- Aggregated feature usage statistics

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Process license key purchases
- Validate license usage
- Understand product adoption and usage trends
- Improve the functionality, performance, and reliability of LogChimp
- Provide customer support
- Communicate important updates

## 3. Self-Hosted Data

LogChimp Self-Managed allows you to host the software within your own infrastructure and retain control over your workspace content, users, and feedback data.

We do not collect, access, or transmit your workspace content, posts, comments, votes, feedback, or personal user information through product telemetry.

We automatically collect limited, non-content technical information from self-managed installations for product analytics, software improvement, and license validation purposes. This may include installation identifiers, license-related information, software versions, deployment environments, and aggregated usage statistics.

Product telemetry is enabled by default.

Error logs or diagnostic information may be collected only if you choose to share them with LogChimp for troubleshooting or support purposes.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share information with:

- Payment processors (for billing)
- Service providers assisting with infrastructure or support
- Authorities if required by law

## 5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to:

- Maintain your account
- Comply with legal obligations
- Resolve disputes

You may request deletion of your account data, subject to legal requirements.

## 6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no system is completely secure. You use the Service at your own risk.

## 7. Your Rights

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data

Requests can be made by contacting us.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.
