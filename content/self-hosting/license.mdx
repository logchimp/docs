LogChimp Self-Managed requires a license key to use any paid feature. This page explains how to purchase a license key, add it to your instance, and manage your subscription.

To learn which features LogChimp offers, read the [features guide](/guide). To compare plans and their support levels, see the [pricing page](https://logchimp.app/pricing/?ref=logchimp-docs).

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

- A Google or GitHub account to sign in to LogChimp Pilot.
- Your billing and card details.
- A [running LogChimp Self-Managed instance](/self-hosting/installation) where you can set environment variables and restart the application.

<Steps>
	<Step>
		## Purchase a license key

		You purchase license keys from [LogChimp Pilot](https://pilot.logchimp.app?ref=logchimp-docs).

		1. Go to [LogChimp Pilot](https://pilot.logchimp.app?ref=logchimp-docs).
		2. Sign in with your Google or GitHub account.
		3. Click **Workspaces** in the left sidebar.
			![LogChimp Pilot workspaces page](/images/docs/self-managed/license/logchimp-pilot-workspaces.png)
		4. Click **Buy license** in the top-right corner.
		5. Select the plan duration: **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
		6. Select the product: **Pro** or **Business**.
		7. Enter your billing and card details.
		8. Complete the checkout.

		After checkout, LogChimp Pilot creates a new workspace for you. It appears in your list of workspaces.

	</Step>
	<Step>
		## Find your license key and RSA public key

		1. Click **Workspaces** in the left sidebar.
		2. Find the new workspace in the list and open it.

		![LogChimp Pilot manage workspace license
		page](/images/docs/self-managed/license/logchimp-pilot-workspace-manage-license.png)

		The page shows two values:

		- License key
		- RSA public key

		You need both values in the next section.

		<Alert type="warning">
			Keep both values private. Do not share them with anyone.
		</Alert>
	</Step>

	<Step>
		## Add the license to your instance

		A license applies to one workspace.

		1. Set these two environment variables in your LogChimp Self-Managed instance:

		| Environment variable | Value |
		| --- | --- |
		| `LOGCHIMP_LICENSE_KEY` | Your license key |
		| `LOGCHIMP_SIGNATURE_TOKEN` | Your RSA public key |

		Example:

		```bash
		LOGCHIMP_LICENSE_KEY=YOUR_LICENSE_KEY
		LOGCHIMP_SIGNATURE_TOKEN=YOUR_RSA_PUBLIC_KEY
		```

		2. Restart the application.

		After the restart, the paid features included in your plan are unlocked. To confirm that the license is active,
		check
		that those features are available in your instance.

		For all available environment variables, see [Environment
		variables](https://docs.logchimp.app/self-hosting/environment-variables#general).
	</Step>

	<Step>
		## Manage your subscription

		You manage your license billing in [LogChimp Pilot](https://pilot.logchimp.app?ref=logchimp-docs). There you can
		renew, cancel, or change your plan.

		Your subscription continues to run until you cancel it or a charge to your card fails.
	</Step>

	<Step>
		## License scope

		A license applies to one workspace. Using one license key in multiple workspaces is against the [LogChimp
		Self-Managed
		Software Subscription
		Agreement](https://logchimp.codecarrot.net/terms/self-managed-software-subscription-agreement-global).
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

You lose access to paid features if:

- The license key is missing.
- The license key is invalid.
- Your subscription has ended because you canceled it or a charge to your card failed.

The error message you see depends on which of these states applies.

If paid features are still locked after you add the license:

1. Check that `LOGCHIMP_LICENSE_KEY` and `LOGCHIMP_SIGNATURE_TOKEN` are set to the values shown on the **Managed License** page.
2. Restart the application.
3. Check that your subscription is active in [LogChimp Pilot](https://pilot.logchimp.app?ref=logchimp-docs).
