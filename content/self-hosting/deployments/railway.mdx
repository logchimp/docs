There are many ways to [install LogChimp](/self-hosting/installation). With Railway, you can get started with just a few easy steps. Click the Deploy button, review the services, and hit deploy. It's that simple.

## Prerequisites

You need a Railway account.

<Steps>
	<Step>
		## 1. Sending emails

		You will need to set-up SMTP configuration to let your users get emails to reset password, and account verification.

		You can read more about [SMTP for Emails](/self-hosting/smtp-for-email).
	</Step>
	<Step>
		## 2. Deploy LogChimp

		Click on the **Deploy on Railway** button.

		<a href="https://railway.com/deploy/logchimp">
			<img src="https://railway.app/button.svg" alt="Deploy on Railway"/>
		</a>
	</Step>
	<Step>
		## 3. Configure the variables

		The configuration page will display all the services which will be deployed as a part of LogChimp to fully function.

		<Alert type="tip">
			You can generates a 32-character secret key by pressing `Command + K` (macOS) or `Ctrl + K` (Windows), or can also enter your own secret key.
		</Alert>

		If you want to add or change existing environment variable for each service, this would be the place before all the services are deployed on Railway.
		To learn more about them, see [environment variables](/self-hosting/environment-variables).

		Enter the following environment variables by clicking on the "Configure" button next to "LogChimp API" service.
		- [LogChimp license key](/self-hosting/license) - To unlock paid features
		- [SMTP email variables](/self-hosting/smtp-for-email) - see **Step 1: Sending emails**

		The services are automatically configured to communicate internally via private networking, except for LogChimp Theme.

		![Railway template configuration page for LogChimp](/images/docs/install/railway/deploy-logchimp-template-on-railway.png)
	</Step>
	<Step>
		## 4. Ready to deploy?

		Once you have configured and reviewed all the services, scroll down and click the "Deploy" button.

		Railway will start creating the services and shows them in your Railway dashboard.

		<Alert type="tip">
			You can also set a custom domain later.
		</Alert>

		When the deployment finishes, you can open your LogChimp site.

		Your LogChimp site is ready to accept **your customers' feedback** with ease. 🎉

		![LogChimp live site preview](/images/docs/install/railway/07_logchimp-live-site-preview.png)
	</Step>
</Steps>

## Unlock more features

Paid plans add features such as [Boards](/guide/boards) and [Roadmaps](/guide/roadmaps). To compare options, see the [paid plans](https://logchimp.app?ref=logchimp-docs).

To unlock these features, [activate your license key](/self-hosting/license).
