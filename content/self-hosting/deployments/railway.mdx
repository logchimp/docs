There are many ways to [install LogChimp](/self-hosting/installation). With Railway, you can get started with just a few easy steps. Click the Deploy button, review the services, and hit deploy. It's that simple.

## Before you start

You need a Railway account.

## Deploy LogChimp

1. Select **Deploy on Railway**.

   <a href="https://railway.com/deploy/logchimp">
   	<img src="https://railway.app/button.svg" alt="Deploy on Railway" />
   </a>

2. Review the services in the template.
3. Select **Deploy**.

Railway starts creating the services and shows them in your Railway dashboard.

Railway pre-configures most of the environment variables. To learn more about them, see [environment variables](/self-hosting/environment-variables).

When the deployment finishes, you can open your LogChimp site. Your LogChimp site is ready to accept **your customers' feedback** with ease. 🎉

![LogChimp live site preview](/images/docs/install/railway/07_logchimp-live-site-preview.png)

## Services the template creates

This template will auto-provision the following services for you:


1. **LogChimp API**: The API that powers your LogChimp site.
2. **LogChimp Theme**: The theme that powers your LogChimp site.
3. **PostgreSQL database**: Stores your LogChimp data, including user information, customer feedback, roadmaps, and boards.
4. **Valkey Caching**: Stores cached data, which helps LogChimp respond faster. It also runs background workers.
5. **Database migration**: Applies pending database migrations. This service does nothing else.

You can change the secret keys and other environment variables.

Railway generates a 32-character secret key when you press `Command + K` (macOS) or `Ctrl + K` (Windows). You can also enter your own secret key.

![LogChimp Railway template](/images/docs/install/railway/01_logchimp-railway-template.png)

## Unlock more features

Paid plans add features such as [Boards](/guide/boards) and [Roadmaps](/guide/roadmaps). To compare options, see the [paid plans](https://logchimp.app?ref=logchimp-docs).

To unlock these features, [activate your license key](/self-hosting/license).
