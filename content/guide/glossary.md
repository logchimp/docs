This page describes some of the terms used in this guide.

- **Post** - A feedback, suggestion, issue submitted by the user.
- **Board** - A way to group a type of posts (e.g. iOS App, Android App)
- **Roadmap** - Tells the status of the feedback (e.g. Backlog, Under review, In progress, Completed)
- **Owner** - The person who setups the LogChimp site _(a.k.a root user)_. By default, the site owner has all the [permissions](/guide/dashboard/role-permission).
