Users can sign up by creating an account on LogChimp to share their feedback or upvote on existing feedback.

<Alert type="tip">
	Want to disable new signups? Know [here](/guide/dashboard/settings).
</Alert>

## Domain Blacklist

Administrators can block users from specific email domains from accessing LogChimp.
This check applies across all user-facing entry points:

- **Sign up**: Users with blacklisted email domains cannot create new accounts.
- **Login**: Existing users with blacklisted email domains are prevented from logging in.
- **Workspace setup**:
The initial workspace setup for LogChimp Self-Managed instances is blocked for blacklisted domains.
- **Email verification**: Email verification is rejected for blacklisted domains.
- **Password reset**: Password reset requests are rejected for blacklisted domains.

<Alert type="warning">
Be very careful when adding domains to the blacklist.

If you include the domain used by the **owner account** email address (e.g., `amce.com`
when the owner email is `admin@amce.com`), the owner will be **permanently prevented from logging in**
until the blacklist is removed or modified.
</Alert>

<Alert type="tip">
**Team members are also affected**: If a team member (dependent) uses an email address belonging to a blacklisted
	domain, they will also be blocked from the above actions.
</Alert>

### Use cases

- **Spam prevention**: Block disposable email providers (e.g., `mailinator.com`, `tempmail.com`)
that spammers commonly use to create throwaway accounts.
- **Competitor protection**:
Prevent known competitor domains from accessing your feedback board and viewing your product roadmap.
- **Compliance**: Enforce organizational policies that restrict access to approved email domains only.
- **Cost control**: Reduce noise and storage overhead by filtering out accounts from domains you don't want to serve.

### Benefits

- **Cleaner community**: Fewer spam accounts means higher-quality feedback and discussions.
- **Reduced maintenance**: Less time spent moderating or deleting abusive accounts.
- **Data privacy**: Keep sensitive product roadmaps and feedback visible only to legitimate users.
- **Zero UI overhead**: Configured once at deployment — no ongoing management needed.

### Configuration

The domain blacklist is configured via the `LOGCHIMP_BLACKLISTED_DOMAINS` environment variable during deployment.
For setup instructions, see [Environment Variables](/self-hosting/environment-variables#api).
