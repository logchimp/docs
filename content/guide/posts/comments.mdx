## Comments

<Alert type="tip">
Comments are available from LogChimp Self-Managed `v0.9.0` onward.

Some features require a paid plan or higher. To compare plans, see the LogChimp [pricing page](https://logchimp.app/pricing).
</Alert>

Comments let you talk directly with your customers. The whole conversation stays in one view.

You can post a comment from the public post page or from the dashboard. To post a comment, you need the `comment:create` permission.

## Private comments

Use a private comment to share information with your team that customers should not see.

<Alert type="tip">
Private comments are available on the **Business** plan.
</Alert>

### Who can see private comments

Private comments do not appear on the public site. You can only see them in the dashboard.

To view a private comment, a team member needs the `comment:view_internal` permission.

### Create a private comment

Before you start, confirm that you have the `comment:create_internal` permission. Without it, you cannot create a private comment.

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Select **Posts** in the sidebar.
3. Open the post you want to comment on.
4. Scroll down the post page.
5. Turn on the **Private comment** toggle.
6. Enter your comment and submit it.
	![Add private comment in a post](/images/guide/posts/add-private-comment-in-posts.webp)

The comment appears in the dashboard and stays hidden from the public site.

![Private comment posted](/images/guide/posts/private-comment-posted.webp)

## FAQ

### How private comments differ from regular comments

|                   | Regular comments                                           | Private comments                                            |
|-------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Who can post      | A user or team member with the `comment:create` permission | A team member with the `comment:create_internal` permission |
| Where they appear | Public site and dashboard                                  | Dashboard only                                              |

### Which plan do I need for private comments?

You need the **Business** plan. To compare plans, see the LogChimp [pricing page](https://logchimp.app/pricing).
