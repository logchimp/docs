You can make changes to the post from the Dashboard.

## Change the post status

You can change the status ([roadmap](/guide/roadmap)) of the post from Dashboard.

![Change post status](/images/guide/posts/modify-post-change-roadmap.webp)

### Notify the upvoters about the status change

You can also send notifications to all the voters of the post about the status change.

<Alert type="tip">
	This feature is available from LogChimp Self-Managed v0.10.0 onboard.
</Alert>

The notifications will be sent via email to the upvoter's registered email address.

LogChimp gives you the option if you would like to send notifications, or not.
