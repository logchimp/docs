## What is LogChimp?

LogChimp helps startups and enterprises collect, prioritize, and act on customer feedback.

Your customers can submit feature requests, issues and vote on them. LogChimp totals the upvotes, so your team can see which ideas have the most impact and decide what to build next that drives revenue with LogChimp.

You can connect LogChimp to your existing system through its [API](/api-reference).
